## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes sales performance data to uncover trends in revenue generation, profitability, customer behavior, and brand performance.

The dashboard was built in Power BI to provide stakeholders with an interactive view of their business performance and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Organizations need visibility into sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, and profit drivers in order to make informed business decisions.

This dashboard consolidates key metrics into a single interactive report, enabling users to monitor performance and identify opportunities for growth.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance
- Analyze profit contribution by brand
- Evaluate customer purchasing trends
- Track revenue generation
- Understand customer segmentation by income level
- Identify popular product characteristics

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Brand Performance
- Apple generated the highest profit among all brands.
- Dell generated the lowest profit among the analyzed brands.

### Customer Segmentation
- High-income customers contributed significantly to overall profitability.
- Profit distribution was relatively balanced across customer income levels.

### Product Preferences
- White-colored products recorded the highest purchase volume.
- Gray products followed closely behind.

### Revenue Performance
- Revenue exceeded 3 million during the reporting period.
- Profit margin remained strong relative to total sales.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

- Increase investment in high-performing brands.
- Introduce targeted marketing campaigns for lower-performing brands.
- Maintain inventory levels for popular product colors.
- Develop loyalty programs for high-value customer segments.
